# AINETOPS IETF 127 Side-Meeting Proposal

**Title:** AINETOPS: Network-Agent Interoperability and Alignment with ICON

**Description:**

This side meeting will bring together operators and implementers to discuss interoperability between independently developed network-management agents and align the proposed AINETOPS work with ICON.

Using operator experience and available IETF 127 hackathon findings, we will examine agent lifecycle management, task exchange, and supporting evidence, clarify overlapping requirements, and identify what existing mechanisms cover and where additional IETF work may be needed. The goal is to converge on a shared problem statement and concrete next steps.

**Area:** Operations and Management (OPS)  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Background:** https://github.com/dan-voyer/IETF/tree/main/AINETOPS-proposal
